“北境探险队，”教授威廉斯解释道给围观的学生们，“这将是我们迄今为止最具挑战性的项目之一。我们将翻译那些在世界外一直未被发现的古老文献。”

托马斯随后中断了关于前往卡恩斯的危险任务的讨论。“教授，我们如何确保我们的翻译准确？”

教授威廉斯笑了笑。“这就是为什么我们需要有才华横溢的语言学家你们这样，”他说。“语言不仅仅是单词，它还包括文化、历史和一个民族的灵魂。”


Maria pondered this question as she flipped through pages of ancient script. “Translation is like building a bridge between two worlds,” Professor Williams explained to the gathered students. “We can’t transport someone completely from one side to the other, but we can help them see across the divide.”

Chapter 3: The Journey Begins

Three weeks later, the expedition team gathered at the train station. Their destination: the remote village of Thornwick, nestled in the Northern mountains. The train journey would take two days, winding through landscapes that seemed to grow more mysterious with each passing mile.

As the train pulled away from the familiar city, Maria watched the urban landscape give way to rolling hills and ancient forests. The further they traveled from civilization, the more she felt like they were journeying not just through space, but through time itself.
